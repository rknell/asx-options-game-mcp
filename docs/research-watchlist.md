## Daily review and records

Read this list during each scheduled news review. Scan for material new catalysts; prioritise BHP and the two broad-market comparators STW/IVV, then examine other names when relevant news appears. Save a `watchlist` section in the existing daily JSON and Markdown logs. Record symbol, timestamped source URLs, event time, catalyst, expected company/fund-specific impact, opposing evidence, already-priced-in considerations, confidence, and status (`no_new_catalyst`, `investigate`, `insufficient_evidence`, or `unavailable`). Do not force a positive or negative score for every name each day.

Keep the Australia/China/global weighted score for the original index experiment separate: those weights are not a validated model for individual stocks or US ETFs. Rank at most two candidates worth further investigation and explain why their news transmission is clearer than the alternatives. Repeated articles about one event count as one catalyst; BHP/RIO and IVV/NDQ overlap must be noted.

Research can continue when game access fails; account values and game availability then remain unknown. Before any future instrument is promoted to an executable strategy, verify its full name, exchange, game eligibility, current spread, trading hours and costs. A larger trading universe would be a separately versioned experiment with explicit sizing and entry/exit rules, not a silent change to news-index-v1. No orders in these additional instruments are authorised by this watchlist alone.

Watchlist observations and any later hypothetical performance must be prospective and timestamped. Do not claim fills, backfill entry prices or select only successful ideas for reporting. Keep personal research logs gitignored; public documentation contains only the generic universe and method.
